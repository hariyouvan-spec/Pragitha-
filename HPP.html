Chapter 01

I Didn't Fall for You at First

When I first saw you, honestly... I didn't feel anything special.

But slowly, I started noticing little things about you.

Your childish behaviour. The way you get excited about small things. The way you tease. Your laugh. Your simplicity. Your innocence.

Somehow, I started looking forward to talking to you.

Waiting for your messages. Remembering little things you said. Thinking about you without even realizing it.

I didn't choose to fall for you.
It just happened.
Slowly. Silently.

And somewhere along the way... I fell for you. ♡

Chapter 02

Somehow, You Became Special

It wasn't one big moment.

It was all the small ones.

Small conversations. Random messages. Teasing each other. Your smile. Those little moments that probably meant nothing to you.

But somehow, they started meaning something to me.

You became a part of my day without even asking for that place.

And before I realized it, you had become someone I genuinely cared about.

Chapter 03

The Day I Finally Told You

I carried those feelings for a long time.

I was scared.

Scared that things might change. Scared that I might lose the friendship we had.

But at some point, I realized that keeping everything inside was harder than being honest.

So I chose honesty over fear.

And I finally told you that I liked you.

Chapter 04

Your Answer

And you told me...

Your words
"You're my good friend."

I understood.

And I respected your feelings.

You didn't do anything wrong.

Sometimes, two people can care about each other and still feel differently.

Chapter 05

Why I Took a Step Back

I didn't step away because I was angry.

I didn't step away to punish you.

You did nothing wrong.

I just needed some time to understand myself.

I wanted to accept your answer properly.

I wanted to learn how to see you simply as my friend.

Sometimes distance isn't about leaving someone.
Sometimes it's about finding yourself again.
Chapter 06

I Tried to See You as a Friend

I really tried.

I told myself that friendship should be enough.

We talked again. We laughed. We teased each other. We had our little conversations.

And for a while, I thought maybe I was finally okay.

But slowly I realized something.

My feelings hadn't disappeared.

That's when I understood that I needed another little distance.

Not because I wanted to lose you.
But because I needed to be honest with myself.

A letter I never knew how to say...
Dear you,

Unna pidikkum nu solla evlo vaati yosichirukken nu unakku theriyadhu.

Aana, every time sollanum nu thonumbodhu, "Maybe nee deserve panra life-ku naan right person illa..." nu en mind dhaan ennai stop pannidum.

Un kooda irukkanum nu aasai irukku. Aana adha vida, nee happy-ah irukkanum nu dhaan romba aasai.

Adhanala, maybe idha naan sollaama irukradhe better.

Unakku oru naal yaaravadhu kidaikkattum... unnoda love-ah full-ah appreciate pannura oruthar.

Andha person naan illa naalum parava illa.

Because naan unna love pannadhu, un life-la enakku oru place venum nu illa.

Nee happy-ah irukkanum nu dhaan.

Maybe you'll never know how much I thought about saying all this.

But at least now, these words don't have to stay inside me anymore.

Love, The person who never said it.
— Haris
